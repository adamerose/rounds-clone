# Run the first loser draft through flow and presentation

Ticket 046 leaves the connected match parked on `ROUND BLUE`. A public inspect trace proves how parked: run the `rematch-draft-replay` profile at seed 41 for 5,466, 5,500, 5,600, 5,700, 5,800, 5,893 and 5,894 ticks and every one of them reports phase `roundBlue` at phase revision 22 with the identical combat digest `7b86de3c86052476…` and byte-identical fighter positions, velocities, health and alive flags. `FlowAuthority::advance` has no match arm for `RoundBlue` or `RoundOrange`, and `apply` answers every `FlowCommand` in those phases with `WrongPhase`, so the round result is a terminal state the authority cannot leave. The source does leave it: 13 ticks later a card silhouette rises behind the overlay, 15 ticks after that the overlay and orange's losing half both vanish, and orange drafts `QUICK SHOT` from a five-card fan before the next arena arrives.

This ticket takes the physics-free prefix of blocked ticket 048 — the draft itself, and nothing after it. It ends at the last frame before the incoming hanging arena's first pixel, so no suspension body, ammunition mark, reload arc, stored jump, wall launch, momentum absorption or projectile recalibration is in scope. Those stay with 048 in its own worktree, where they are blocked on a hanging-arena suspension fit this ticket does not touch.

Why it matters: the coverage ledger currently calls the 04:10–04:20 draft unresolved and `S1-flow-draft`/`S5-card-combat` are the two slices with the least footage behind them. This interval is the whole of a card draft — offers, reveal cadence, two hovers, one confirmation, a loadout append and a half reset — and it needs no new arena, transport or renderer path to deliver. It is not quite physics-free, and the ticket says so rather than rounding it off: registering `QUICK SHOT` as implemented gives the private `spawn_bullet` boundary a per-fighter launch-speed argument in place of its unconditional `BULLET_SPEED`, so the authority can hand each shot its owner's own launch speed. That is a signature change, not a physics change — no constant, body, collider, solver setting, damping term or arena moves, and `BULLET_SPEED` stays 3,600 for every fighter.
