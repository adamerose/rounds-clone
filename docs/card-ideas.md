# Card ideas

The pool of mechanics QUARREL can draw from: every good idea from ROUNDS, plus our own.
Nothing here is committed to the MVP; it is a list to pick from and add to.
★ marks favourites Adam has named.

ROUNDS names are listed only so we know where an idea came from.
Shipped cards get our own names, text and art, and their numbers come from our own tuning.
Summaries come from the project's earlier catalog of 67 ROUNDS cards (`research/notes/cards.md`, full records at `archive/godot-csharp-prototype-2026-09-03:spec/cards.json`).

## Every card should be easy to read

- One sentence explains it, and you can see it working the first time it fires.
- Upsides and downsides are both obvious: "much harder hits, much slower fire".
- No hidden recipes; anything that combines tells you so on the card.

## From ROUNDS

### Shots that change how they travel

- ★ **Grow:** shots get bigger and hit harder the farther they fly (ROUNDS: Grow).
- ★ **Steer:** you guide your shot in flight with your aim; shots are slower (ROUNDS: Remote).
- **Homing:** shots curve toward opponents in sight; less damage, slower fire (ROUNDS: Homing).
- **Hug the floor:** shots bend to avoid the ground (ROUNDS: Sneaky).
- **Fast shot:** much faster shots; with a damage boost and slower fire it makes a sniper (ROUNDS: Quick Shot, Fastball, Wind Up, Steady Shot).
- **Big shots:** a bigger hitbox (ROUNDS: Big Bullets).

### Shots that bounce or go through things

- ★ **Bounce:** shots rebound off surfaces a few more times (ROUNDS: Bouncy).
- **Chaos bounce:** many more bounces, a little less damage (ROUNDS: Mayhem).
- **Bank shot:** each bounce adds damage (ROUNDS: Trickster).
- **Seeking bounce:** a bounce redirects toward an opponent in sight (ROUNDS: Target Bounce).
- **Slow on bounce:** shots lose speed when they bounce, so they linger (ROUNDS: Riccochet).
- ★ **Drill:** shots pass through terrain for a short distance (ROUNDS: Drill Ammo).

### How many shots

- **Spread:** five shots in a fan with long range (ROUNDS: Barrage).
- **Shotgun:** five shots in a short-range fan (ROUNDS: Buckshot).
- **Burst:** one pull fires three shots in a row (ROUNDS: Burst).
- ★ **Spray:** a very fast, low-damage stream (ROUNDS: Spray).
- **Combine:** fewer, much harder shots (ROUNDS: Combine, Careful Planning).

### What a hit does

- **Explode:** shots explode on impact; slower fire (ROUNDS: Explosive Bullet).
- **Delayed bomb:** a hit leaves a bomb that goes off a moment later (ROUNDS: Timed Detonation).
- ★ **Poison:** a hit's damage arrives over a few seconds (ROUNDS: Poison, Parasite).
- **Toxic cloud:** hits leave a damaging, slowing cloud (ROUNDS: Toxic Cloud).
- **Stun:** hits stagger the opponent (ROUNDS: Dazzle).
- **Chill:** hits slow the opponent (ROUNDS: Cold Bullets).
- **Thrust:** shots keep pushing whatever they hit (ROUNDS: Thruster).
- ★ **Reload on hit:** dealing damage reloads your gun (ROUNDS: Scavenger).
- **Lifesteal:** damage you deal heals you (ROUNDS: Leech, Taste of Blood).
- **Block back:** dealing damage refreshes your block (ROUNDS: Refresh).

### What a block does

ROUNDS' richest combos come from reactions to blocking.

- ★ **Teleport:** blocking moves you forward, even through walls (ROUNDS: Teleport).
- ★ **Echo:** each block happens a second time a moment later (ROUNDS: Echo).
- ★ **Radar:** blocking fires a shot at an opponent in sight (ROUNDS: Radar Shot).
- ★ **Supernova:** blocking pulls everything in, then stuns (ROUNDS: Supernova).
- **Push:** blocking shoves nearby opponents away (ROUNDS: Shockwave).
- **Pull:** blocking drags nearby opponents in (ROUNDS: Implode).
- **Bombs:** blocking scatters bombs (ROUNDS: Bombs Away).
- **Saw:** blocking spins a damaging saw around you (ROUNDS: Saw).
- **Ring of shots:** blocking fires a ring of weak slowing shots (ROUNDS: EMP).
- **Charge:** blocking launches you forward (ROUNDS: Shield Charge).
- **Reload:** blocking reloads instantly (ROUNDS: Tactical Reload).
- **Empower:** blocking charges your next shot, which can trigger block effects where it lands (ROUNDS: Empower).
- **Fields:** blocking leaves a healing area, a damaging slow area, or a short silence on nearby opponents (ROUNDS: Healing Field, Static Field, Silence).
- **Max-health blast:** blocking damages nearby opponents based on your max health (ROUNDS: Overpower).

### Your body and survival

- ★ **Countdown:** standing still charges a field that pulls, damages and triggers your block effects (ROUNDS: Abyssal Countdown).
- **Chase:** moving toward an opponent makes you faster (ROUNDS: Chase).
- **Cold aura:** nearby opponents are slowed (ROUNDS: Chilling Presence).
- **Drain aura:** nearby opponents are drained to heal you (ROUNDS: Lifestealer).
- **Delayed damage:** damage you take arrives over time (ROUNDS: Decay).
- **Second life:** revive once per fight, with less max health (ROUNDS: Phoenix).
- **Full-health shield:** much more max health while you are nearly unhurt (ROUNDS: Pristine Perseverence).
- **Hit surge:** dealing damage briefly raises your max health (ROUNDS: Brawler).
- **Auto block:** firing your last shot blocks automatically (ROUNDS: Shields Up).
- **Reload pulses:** reloading sends out damaging pulses (ROUNDS: Radiance).
- **Pay in blood:** firing costs health instead of waiting (ROUNDS: Demonic Pact).

### Plain trades

- **Glass cannon:** double damage, far less health (ROUNDS: Glass Cannon).
- **Tank:** double health, slower fire and reload (ROUNDS: Tank).
- **Bulk:** more health (ROUNDS: Huge, Defender).
- **Fast hands:** much faster reload (ROUNDS: Quick Reload, Fast Forward).

## Our own

### Movement

- ★ **Grapple hook:** fire a line that pulls you to terrain, or pulls a light opponent to you.
- **Dash:** a short burst in your aim direction with a moment of invulnerability.
- **Double jump.**
- **Jetpack:** hold jump to hover briefly.
- **Ground slam:** drop fast and knock back whatever is near where you land.
- **Rewind:** jump back to where you were two seconds ago.
- **Swap:** your hit swaps places with the target.

### Weapons that replace the gun

- ★ **Portal gun:** two portals that shots and fighters pass through.
- ★ **Melee:** a sword or bat that swings, deflects shots and knocks hard.
- **Beam:** hold to charge, release a piercing laser.
- **Boomerang:** shots come back to you.
- **Mortar:** shots arc under gravity and break terrain.
- **Mines:** place sticky charges you detonate.

### Shots

- **Split:** a shot splits in two when it bounces.
- **Orbit:** shots circle you before they fly.
- **Chain:** a hit arcs to the nearest other target.
- **Black hole:** shots end in a small pull field.
- **Magnet:** shots pull light objects and opponents slightly as they pass.
- **Freeze:** shots that hit terrain leave ice blocks you can stand on.
- **Builder:** shots that hit nothing become a short-lived platform.

### Body

- **Shrink:** smaller and harder to hit, but lighter and easier to knock out.
- **Heavy:** barely knocked back, slower.
- **Decoy:** blocking leaves a fake you behind.
- **Vanish:** briefly invisible after a block.

## Duo cards

A duo card appears in your offer only once you hold both of its parent cards, and it shows both parent icons so the reason is obvious.

- **Bounce + Explode:** every bounce explodes.
- **Grow + Drill:** shots grow as they bore through terrain and leave a wider tunnel.
- **Steer + Spray:** every shot in the stream follows your aim.
- **Teleport + Echo:** a second teleport follows the first, so you can blink twice.
- **Poison + Reload on hit:** each poison tick also counts as a hit.

## Trait cards

You hold one trait at a time; taking a new trait replaces your old one.
A trait changes your whole fighter, so it gives a build its direction.

- **Sniper:** shots fly far faster and hit harder; you fire much less often.
- **Juggernaut:** heavy and hard to knock back, but slow.
- **Acrobat:** a second air jump and faster wall climbing, less health.
- **Gunslinger:** two guns that fire alternately.
- **Brawler:** melee instead of a gun, with a dash.
- **Ghost:** pass through thin platforms, take a little more damage.

## Curses

Curses appear now and then as a card type in the loser's offer.
Taking one spends your pick on hurting the leader instead of helping yourself.

- **Slippery:** the cursed fighter slides on every floor.
- **Butterfingers:** smaller magazine.
- **Lead boots:** lower jump.
- **Marked:** takes more damage and glows.
- **Slow block:** longer block cooldown.
- **Magnet:** your shots curve slightly toward the cursed fighter.

Curses that remove control (reversed controls, random misfires) are excluded because they make losing feel unfair.

## Fight modifiers

Every few fights, at random, one rule applies to everyone for that fight and is announced before it starts.

- Low gravity.
- Ice floor.
- Everyone's shots bounce once more.
- Everyone's shots are huge.
- Wind that pushes shots and fighters.
- The floor slowly crumbles.
- Lights out: only muzzle flashes and shots are lit.
- Double speed.
